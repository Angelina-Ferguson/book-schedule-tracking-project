Plans for shelf schduler and what it will do:
1. allow readers to import their TBR books as well as any data from kindle/ libby or library
2. allow readers to "schedule" these books.
    this could be scheduled based on availability on libby as a book they have on hold (this could be estimated)
    could be scheduled based on user preference
    could be scheduled based on pricing? maybe if the user says they want to budget a certain amount of money for their books monthly (could factor in digital and physical)
    can also factor in book releases or shipping arrivals
3. allow the reader to adapt their schedule based on things that worked/didn't work. when schedules are created, factor in the following
    excitement to read the book (star or 1-5 rank)
    dnf frequency or duration before dnf (this should be the shortest amount of time the book can be scheduled for)
    book duration + average reading pace (if logged, otherwise estimate based on book size in pages / days of completion)
    allow for asynchronously reading multiple books at a time. maybe give reminders to untouched books ("you havent picked this one up in a while")


consider future ways this tool could be used for people who are in a reading slump
- allow the reader to mark books that are similar and whether they like or dislike that. 
- compute a dnf frequency based on genre, author, or any other valuable metrics
    if a user reaches a specific genre related frequency, offer alternative options or other genres they can try.
